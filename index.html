FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our travel services.

You can book a flight by filling out the booking form on our website, calling us at +91 91159-33586, or visiting our office in Mandi Gobindgarh. Our team will find the best deals and confirm your booking within hours.

We provide visa assistance for major destinations including USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Schengen countries, Singapore, Dubai, Thailand, and many more. Our visa experts guide you through the entire documentation process.

We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), debit cards, and credit cards. All digital payments are processed through secure, encrypted gateways.

Absolutely! All our tour packages are fully customizable. You can choose your destinations, hotels, activities, and duration. Our travel consultants will create a personalized itinerary based on your preferences and budget.

Cancellation policies vary depending on the service (flights, hotels, visas). Generally, flights follow airline cancellation policies, while hotels offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. We always inform you of the cancellation terms before booking confirmation.

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance plans that cover medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost baggage, flight delays, and more. We recommend travel insurance for all international trips for complete peace of mind.

For the best deals and availability, we recommend booking international trips 4-6 weeks in advance and domestic trips 1-2 weeks in advance. Visa processing can take 2-4 weeks depending on the country, so plan accordingly.
